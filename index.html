My story

I was born logical. Spirit had other plans.

I grew up in a family that valued academia. My grandparents were university professors, and I was the logical, studious one. I went to film school and learned to see the world through a camera lens.

Then I started having intuitive experiences I couldn't explain away. I followed them, and they led me to tarot, to psychic readings, and eventually to reading for thousands of people.

My own love life was a different story. For years I was caught in my head about spirituality, deep in every rabbit hole online, anxious and overthinking. I went through the dark night of the soul: painful rejection, runners who ran hot and cold, and a long, hard separation. I invested thousands of dollars in healers, coaches and programs. Some worked. Some didn't.

What changed everything was real support, and learning to love myself. I stopped waiting to be chosen and started choosing myself.

More than a year before I met my husband, one of my psychic mentors told me he was coming. When he arrived, it felt peaceful, natural and healing. We had real obstacles between us, and we chose love anyway, again and again.

That's what I've learned true love is: two people who keep choosing it.

Since 2019, I've given over 4,000 readings, and now I help spiritual women do the same inner work that changed my life. I'm not here to rescue you. I'm here to be your lighthouse while you steer your own ship home.
